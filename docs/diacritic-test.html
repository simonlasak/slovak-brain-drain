Slovenská diakritika — test rezov

This page exists to verify that every font we ship to production renders the full set of Slovak diacritics — ä č ď ô ľ ŕ š ť ž — cleanly at every weight we use. Visual judgement is required: do the marks sit on the cap line correctly, do they collide with ascenders, do they look balanced in ALL CAPS, and is the overall texture acceptable.

Visual judgement checklist for Šimon

  • Do ä, ô sit cleanly on the cap line in Source Serif 4, or do they look pinched against the ascenders?
  • Are ľ, ŕ, ď, ť visually distinct in JetBrains Mono at 400 weight, or do their háčik/cárka marks merge?
  • In ALL CAPS at 14px (ČESKÝ KORIDOR), is the 0.12em letter-spacing enough to keep diacritics from crowding their neighbour?
  • Does Inter Tight at 500 weight feel close enough to a UI sans companion to Source Serif 4, or does the texture mismatch?
  • Is the hero stat (240 000) recognisably tabular and balanced at 96px / 600 weight in Source Serif 4?
  • Does the Slovak phrase «príliš žltučký kôň úpel ďábelské ódy» render every diacritic correctly, with no missing glyphs or font-substitution fallbacks?
  • If we reject Inter Tight here, the only fallback we can take is licensing Pangram Sans — plain Inter is excluded by spec.
